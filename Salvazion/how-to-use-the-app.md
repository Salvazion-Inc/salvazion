# How to use the Platform

You have an account. Now live it: one pillar at a time, one day at a time, with the **Community** and **Salvazion AI** beside you.

Open the Hub: [https://salvazion.org/hub/dashboard](https://salvazion.org/hub/dashboard)

## The Hub (Dashboard)

The Hub is home. Scores for Salvation, Health and Freedom. Today's agenda. The next faithful action — not another feed.

Start here every morning. Close here every night. Discipline is a calendar, not a mood.

Prayer does **not** live on the Dashboard. Open **Salvation → Prayer** to write, edit, reorder by priority and pray.

## Salvation — awakened soul

* **Bible** — full offline library (Spanish, English, Portuguese and originals). Read, search, concordance. The Word first — not cross-less mindfulness.
* **Devotional** — a daily text aligned to Scripture, virtue and BioConservatism.
* **Prayer** — motives you can **edit** and **rank by priority**. Start a **prayer session** and walk the list in order. Mark prayed or answered.
* **Score** — streaks and measurable spiritual consistency. What is not measured dissolves.

Open: [salvazion.org/hub/bible](https://salvazion.org/hub/bible) · [salvazion.org/hub/bible?tab=prayer](https://salvazion.org/hub/bible?tab=prayer) · [salvazion.org/hub/devotional](https://salvazion.org/hub/devotional)

## Health — temple in motion

* Log sleep, hydration, sun, meals and sport.
* Use **phone sensors** and **Bluetooth heart rate**.
* Photo tools: cineanthropometry, meal vision and sleep quality.
* Sync devices and wearables for biomarkers. **Premium** deepens the cloud stack (Fitbit, Oura, WHOOP, Garmin), clinical record and women's health.

This is practical BioConservatism. The body is not hardware to upgrade. It is a covenant.

Open: [salvazion.org/hub/health](https://salvazion.org/hub/health)
