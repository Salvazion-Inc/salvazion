Experts describe this as a "major crisis," where governments and corporations could puppet the network, morphing Bitcoin into a centralized entity.

In 2025, this re-centralization undermines Bitcoin's promise of censorship resistance, as institutional players like BlackRock amplify their sway through financial levers.
